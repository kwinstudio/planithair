Before you book.

How do I request an appointment?+

Complete the form above. WhatsApp opens with your treatment, date, time and name ready to send.

How early should I arrive?+

Please arrive no more than 10 minutes early so the previous appointment can finish comfortably.

How should I prepare my hair?+

Please arrive with clean, detangled hair unless another preparation has been agreed beforehand.

Can I bring someone with me?+

Space is limited, so please keep guests to a minimum unless agreed in advance.
